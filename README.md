# SYE

This is a collection of shiny apps that I built during my senior year experience using a data set about Collegiate Sports. My SYE allowed me to explore any direction of data science and I choose to further explore visualizations though shiny apps. I sorted through this data set about college sports and started by creating visualizations to explore revenue to expenditure at different schools, comparing it over time, and breaking it down by sport and gender - this is all contained in Exploration.qmd. BySchool and BySport are provide more detailed plots of revenue and expenditure broken down by either school or sport over a specified time period.
